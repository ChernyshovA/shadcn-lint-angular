Add support for Angular templates: static `class`, `*Class`, `routerLinkActive` and `style` attributes in `.html` files and inline templates, with the component's own styles and the project's global styles from `angular.json` or Nx `project.json` known to `no-unknown-classes`, and a warning when they cannot be read. What is read the Angular way is read in Angular code: templates, scripts that import from `@angular/`, and the other scripts of an Angular project, a project of `angular.json` or an Nx `project.json` with a target Angular builds or a `prefix`. React, Vue and Svelte files, and files outside an Angular project, lint as they do with `@shadcn/lint` 0.2.1, except for the fixes of upstream bugs shadcn-ui/lint#105 (SVG `stop-color` and the like), shadcn-ui/lint#108 (the `style` condition when a stylesheet imports a package) and shadcn-ui/lint#111 (`tsconfig.base.json`), and for the new settings `settings.shadcn.entryPoint` and `no-restyle`'s `multipleContracts`, which change nothing unless they are set.
